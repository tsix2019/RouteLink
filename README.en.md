<h1 align="center">RouteLink</h1>

<p align="center"><a href="README.md">中文</a> | <b>English</b></p>

> 🚧 **Work in progress**: the first milestone (M1) is under development. Features, screenshots and downloads will be added as they land.

RouteLink is a mobile app for managing OpenWrt routers on iOS and Android, available in English and Chinese.

- **Liquid Glass UI**: uses the native iOS 26 glass effects on iPhone, and recreates the same look on Android with background blur.
- **Auto-discovery**: scans your LAN for OpenWrt devices, including side routers that aren't the default gateway.
- **Multiple routers**: add several routers and switch between them at any time.
- **Feature parity**: aims to cover everything existing open-source OpenWrt apps do. High-risk operations such as flashing firmware or factory reset come with tiered warnings.
- **Private by design**: no analytics or tracking. The app talks only to your router.

Design document (Chinese): [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-routelink-design.md](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-routelink-design.md)
